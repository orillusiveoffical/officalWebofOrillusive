# Orillusive — Official Website Specification & Prompt Guide

> **Studio:** Orillusive  
> **Position:** Premium Software Engineering Studio  
> **Tagline:** Engineering Digital Products That Matter.  
> **Status:** Production Specification  

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Mission

- **Company Name:** Orillusive
- **Tagline:** Engineering Digital Products That Matter.
- **Positioning:** Premium Software Engineering Studio
- **What We Do:** We design and build modern software that solves real business problems. We focus on long-term digital products instead of short-term solutions.
- **Brand Impression:** When someone visits the website, they should immediately think: *"This company builds serious software."*

---

## 2. Inspired References

- **Google Antigravity:** Elegant color palette, premium gradients, lighting, glass effects, and futuristic atmosphere.
- **Notion:** Clean layouts, spacing, typography hierarchy, section structure, and readability.
- **Bugatti:** Cinematic fullscreen hero section, smooth animations, storytelling, transitions, and luxury experience.
- **Ferrari:** Splash screen and loading experience inspiration (dark screen, animated logo, 2–3s duration, smooth fade transition).
- **Lamborghini:** Premium navigation bar, minimal floating menu, glass effect, rounded, spacing, and interaction design.
- **Ibex:** Recreate a similar "industries / products / capabilities" section using Orillusive's actual work.

*Note: All design elements are original identities inspired by these world-class experiences without copying content.*

---

## 3. Strict Guidelines & Exclusions

- ❌ **No AI References:** Never mention OpenAI, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Machine Learning, Generative AI, or any AI company names. Do NOT present Orillusive as an AI company.
- ❌ **No Fake Credibility:** Never create fake clients, fake testimonials, fake awards, or fake statistics.
- ❌ **No Clichés:** No startup clichés, no flashy gradients, no colorful illustrations, no oversized emojis, no generic agency templates.
- ✨ **Craftsmanship:** Everything should feel handcrafted, minimal, luxury, elegant, modern, confident, premium, future-ready, and engineering-focused.

---

## 4. Color Palette

- **Primary Background:** `#F7F7F5` (Soft luxury light background)
- **Dark Sections:** `#111111` (Deep black contrast sections)
- **Primary Text:** Near Black (`#111111` / `#1A1A1A` on light, `#F7F7F5` on dark)
- **Accent:** `#4F6B85` (Steel Slate Blue)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#2C1E16` (Dark Brown)
- **Cards:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with very soft shadows and subtle borders
- **Gradients:** Soft premium gradients inspired by Google Antigravity lighting.

---

## 5. Typography & Aesthetics

- **Headline Typography:** Clean sans-serif font (`Outfit` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`)
- **Body Typography:** Minimal paragraphs, generous spacing (`Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`)
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace, Notion-inspired breathing room.

## 6. Website Architecture & Section Structure

### 1. Loader / Splash Screen (Ferrari Inspired)
- Dark screen (`#111111`).
- Animated Orillusive emblem logo.
- Elegant loading progress / pulse.
- Duration: 2–3 seconds with smooth fade transition.

### 2. Navbar (Lamborghini Inspired)
- Floating, rounded, glassmorphic container with blur.
- Links: Logo, Services, Products, About, Process, Contact.
- Primary CTA Button: "Book a Discovery Call".
- Smooth dynamic state change on scroll.

- Fullscreen layout with luxury background lighting & subtle floating particles.
